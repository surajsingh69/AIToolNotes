## Content Marketing Workflow

**Week 1: Planning**
1. Research keywords with AnswerThePublic and Google Trends
2. Identify content gaps with BuzzSumo
3. Create editorial calendar in Notion

**Week 2: Creation**
1. Write content with Grammarly + Copy.ai
2. Create visuals with Canva AI
3. Optimize for SEO with Ubersuggest

**Week 3: Publishing**
1. Schedule social posts with Buffer
2. Set up email campaign with Mailchimp
3. Publish and promote

**Week 4: Analysis**
1. Review analytics in Google Analytics
2. Check heatmaps in Hotjar
3. Identify top-performing content
4. Plan next month's content

## Content Types That Work

1. **How-to guides** - High search intent, easy to rank
2. **Listicles** - Shareable, easy to scan
3. **Comparisons** - Commercial intent, high conversion
4. **Case studies** - Builds trust, generates leads
5. **Video content** - Engages audience, improves SEO

## Distribution Strategy

**Owned Media:**
- Blog (primary)
- Email newsletter
- Social media profiles

**Earned Media:**
- Guest posts on relevant blogs
- Reddit/Quora answers with value
- Community engagement (forums, Discord)

**Shared Media:**
- Social sharing buttons
- Content syndication (with canonical tags)
- Influencer outreach

## Measurement

Track these metrics weekly:
- Organic traffic (Google Analytics)
- Search rankings (Google Search Console)
- Email subscribers (Mailchimp)
- Social engagement (Buffer/Hootsuite)
- Content shares (manual tracking)
