## Customer Service Best Practices

1. **Respond fast** - 78% of customers expect a response within 1 hour
2. **Be human** - AI helps, but human touch matters
3. **Document everything** - Use knowledge bases to reduce repeat questions
4. **Collect feedback** - Survey customers regularly
5. **Personalize** - Use customer data to tailor responses

## Common Customer Service AI Mistakes

1. **Over-relying on chatbots** - 86% of customers want human option
2. **Slow response times** - AI can't replace fast human response
3. **Generic responses** - Personalize every interaction
4. **Not measuring satisfaction** - Track CSAT, NPS, response time
5. **Ignoring channels** - Meet customers where they are
