## Free AI Tools for Content Marketing

### 1. Copy.ai Free
**Best for:** AI marketing copy generation

Copy.ai offers:
- 2,000 words/month
- Blog post generation
- Social media content

- Word limits
- Generic outputs
- No advanced templates

## Common Marketing AI Mistakes

1. **Over-relying on AI for copy** - AI drafts, humans refine.
2. **Ignoring data** - Analytics guide decisions, not guesses.
3. **Inconsistent posting** - Schedule consistently with Buffer.
4. **Not testing** - A/B test everything.
5. **Ignoring mobile** - 70%+ of traffic is mobile.
