## Common Mistakes

1. **Ignoring licensing terms** - Free tiers often have commercial use restrictions. Check before using for client work.

2. **Expecting human-level quality** - AI voices are good but not perfect. Use for drafts, not final products.

3. **Not testing multiple tools** - Different tools excel at different use cases. Test 3-4 before committing.

4. **Forgetting about SSML** - Many tools support Speech Synthesis Markup Language for better control over pronunciation and pacing.

## Voice Quality Tips

1. **Use punctuation** - Commas, periods, and exclamation marks dramatically affect pacing and naturalness.

2. **Break up long text** - Short paragraphs sound more natural than walls of text.

3. **Test different voices** - Some voices work better for specific content types.

4. **Adjust speed and pitch** - Fine-tune settings for your use case.

5. **Add pauses** - Use SSML or natural breaks to improve flow.
