# AI Script to Video Workflow: Complete Guide

AI script-to-video tools can turn written text into finished videos with AI voices, stock footage, and automatic editing. The best AI script-to-video workflows combine scriptwriting, voiceover, visual generation, and editing into a streamlined pipeline. We tested the top AI script-to-video tools and workflows to help you create videos faster without filming or complex editing software.

## Best AI Script-to-Video Workflows

**1. ChatGPT + Pika Labs + CapCut**
- **Script:** ChatGPT writes the script from your outline
- **Visuals:** Pika Labs generates B-roll and scenes from prompt
- **Voiceover:** ElevenLabs or CapCut TTS generates narration
- **Edit:** CapCut assembles footage, adds captions, and exports
- **Best for:** YouTube explainers and social media content
- **Cost:** Free tiers cover most needs

**2. HeyGen + Canva + CapCut**
- **Script:** Write or paste script into HeyGen
- **Visuals:** HeyGen AI avatar presents the video
- **Graphics:** Canva creates titles, thumbnails, and graphics
- **Edit:** CapCut adds music, captions, and final touches
- **Best for:** Training videos and faceless channels
- **Cost:** HeyGen free tier includes 5 minutes/month

**3. Synthesia + PowerPoint + Camtasia**
- **Script:** Import script into Synthesia
- **Visuals:** AI presenter delivers the script with slides
- **Slides:** PowerPoint creates presentation visuals
- **Edit:** Camtasia records and edits the final video
- **Best for:** Corporate training and e-learning
- **Cost:** Synthesia free tier limited; Camtasia has free trial

**4. Descript + Pika + Canva**
- **Script:** Write script in Descript
- **Visuals:** Pika generates supporting visuals
- **Voiceover:** Descript AI voice clones or stock voices
- **Graphics:** Canva creates thumbnails and end screens
- **Best for:** Podcasts and interview-style videos
- **Cost:** Descript free tier; Pika free credits

**5. Runway ML + Audacity + Premiere Pro**
- **Script:** Write script and record rough audio in Audacity
- **Visuals:** Runway ML generates AI video clips and effects
- **Audio:** Audacity cleans up audio and adds music
- **Edit:** Premiere Pro assembles final video with AI assists
- **Best for:** Creative and artistic video projects
- **Cost:** Runway free tier; Audacity free; Premiere Pro subscription

## How to Build Your AI Script-to-Video Pipeline

1. **Start with a script outline.** Use ChatGPT to expand your outline into a full script with timing notes.
2. **Generate visuals.** Use Pika or DALL-E 3 to create B-roll and scene visuals from script prompts.
3. **Create voiceover.** Use ElevenLabs or CapCut TTS to generate narration from the script.
4. **Assemble in editor.** Import visuals and audio into CapCut or Descript.
5. **Add polish.** Use AI captions, color correction, and music.
6. **Export and publish.** Export in platform-specific settings.

## Common Script-to-Video Mistakes

1. **Writing scripts that are too long.** AI voiceovers and short-form platforms prefer concise scripts.
2. **Ignoring pacing.** AI voices can sound monotone; vary sentence length and add pauses.
3. **Using generic prompts.** Specific prompts produce better AI video. Include style, lighting, and composition.
4. **Skipping review.** AI voices and video can have errors. Always watch the final export.
5. **Forgetting about music.** Background music sets tone. Use royalty-free libraries.

## Tips for AI Script-to-Video

1. **Write for the ear, not the eye.** Scripts sound better than they read.
2. **Use B-roll generously.** Cover voiceover with relevant visuals every 3-5 seconds.
3. **Add captions.** 85% of social video is watched without sound.
4. **Test different AI voices.** Find a voice that matches your content tone.
5. **Batch create.** Write multiple scripts and generate videos in batches.
