## Pro Tips

1. **Generate, then edit:** AI drafts are starting points, not final copy
2. **Test variations:** Generate 10 headlines, pick the best 2
3. **Maintain brand voice:** Train AI on your best-performing copy
4. **Combine tools:** Use Copy.ai for ideas, Grammarly for polish
5. **Track performance:** A/B test AI copy vs human copy

## Conclusion

Free AI copywriting tools in 2026 make professional copy accessible to everyone. Start with free tiers, master prompt engineering, and scale with paid plans as your needs grow.
