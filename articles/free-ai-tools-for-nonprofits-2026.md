1. **Ignoring nonprofit discounts.** Many paid tools offer free or discounted plans for nonprofits.
2. **Using too many disconnected tools.** Integrate tools where possible to reduce admin overhead.
3. **Neglecting donor privacy.** Review data handling and compliance with donor protection laws.
4. **Overlooking impact measurement.** Use Google Forms and analytics to track program outcomes.
5. **Forgetting to train staff and volunteers.** Ensure everyone can use the tools effectively.
