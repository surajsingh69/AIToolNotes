# Free AI Study Tools (2026)
**Tools tested for actual studying: flashcards, lecture summaries, research, and exam prep.**

| Tool | Best For | Free Tier |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **Quizlet AI** | Flashcards, test prep | Limited AI-generated sets |
| **NotebookLM** | PDF/lecture summaries | 100 notebooks, 50 sources each |
| **Perplexity** | Research with citations | Free with limits |
| **Anki + AnkiConnect** | Spaced repetition | Free, open-source |
| **Claude** | Essay writing, coding | Free tier available |
| **Photomath** | Math/science solving | Free for basic problems |

## Study Stack Recommendation
1. **NotebookLM** — upload PDFs and lectures, get summaries and audio overviews
2. **Perplexity** — research with cited sources
3. **Quizlet AI** — flashcards and practice tests
4. **Claude** — essay drafts and concept explanations
